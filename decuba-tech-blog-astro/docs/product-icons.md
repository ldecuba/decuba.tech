# Post thumbnail product badges

Badges on `/blog` use the primary category, falling back to `category`. The mapping lives in `src/lib/category-icons.ts`. The main thumbnail image and post data stay unchanged.

Coverage: 35 of 38 editor categories.

No exact verified logo added for: AI, Exchange, Microsoft Security. These categories keep their thumbnail without an extra product badge; no unrelated product is substituted.
