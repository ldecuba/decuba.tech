The interesting work starts after a Power Platform demo ends. That is where permissions, ownership and the small operational decisions decide whether an idea becomes something people can actually use.

## Where the friction lives

The platform keeps getting more capable, but capability does not remove the need for a good path through the work. The best solutions make the next action obvious without pretending the underlying process is simple.

## My current read

The progress is real. I am more interested in the boring edges than the launch headline, because that is where a team feels the difference every day.
